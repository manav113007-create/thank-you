A little something
for everyone
who remembered.

Scroll to open it ↓

To everyone who wished me —

to everyone who took a moment out of their day to send a message, make a call, or just think of me —

to the ones who called instead of texting, because it felt more like you —

to the ones who sent the same three-word message and somehow it still meant something —

to the group chats, the stories, the tags, the memes made just for the occasion —

none of it went unnoticed. I read every one of them, more than once.

THANK YOU ❤️

You genuinely made today feel like something worth celebrating.
